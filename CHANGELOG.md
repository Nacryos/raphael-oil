# Raphael Oil changelog

## 0.5.0 (2026-11-14): first public release
- Built from the Oil Pass 0.4.2 textures exactly; nothing repainted.
- Two downloads: `Raphael-Oil-0.5.0.zip` (colour textures, for most players) and `Raphael-Oil-0.5.0-PBR.zip`
  (adds the LabPBR normal and specular maps; the relief and sheen show only with a shader pack).
- Mojang's painting artworks are left out (the game's own paintings show).
- Blockstate files that only repeated the vanilla game's are left out.
- PNGs are losslessly recompressed (identical pixels).
- README, LICENSE and CREDITS are included.

## 0.4.2 (2026-11-13)
- Nothing floats over the two-block plants. The small dripleaf's V marks are gone, and the rose bush, lilac, peony and tall
  seagrass are fixed the same way.

## 0.4.1 (2026-11-13)
- Tall grass, tall flowers, ferns and the pitcher crop stand on the ground as one plant two blocks tall.

## 0.4 (2026-11-12)
- Every block is painted on a torus, so a block can no longer show a seam against its neighbour.

## 0.3 (2026-11-12)
- Ores, moss, cracked bricks and grass sides are painted on their base block's own paint, so they meet their neighbours
  without a line.
- Each ore has 3 painted variants.

## 0.2 (2026-11-12)
- Loose oil strokes over nearly every block, item, mob, particle and GUI texture (the Oil Pass).
- Tiling is checked in the game's own layout.

## 0.1 (2026-11-11 / 12)
- The first pack: every block painted with Raphael's oil-paint strokes instead of pixel-drawn.
- The first oil-pass zip.
